Seasons and weather. The year has nine seasons, from blossom time and firefly nights to shooting stars, falling leaves and misty days, and the sky changes every few hours: sunshine, clouds, rain, storms, snow and fog, the same for everyone on an island. Blobs feel it: they keep in when it rains, head out in the snow, and doze through a summer afternoon's heat. The season and the sky show at the top of the screen.
