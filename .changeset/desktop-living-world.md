The private blob lives its random life on the device, catching up on the time the app was closed. Blobs are played back from their stored timelines, couples wander together, children are drawn smaller, and each blob wears a sign of its sex — a bow or a bowler hat, measured onto every silhouette. Sex and attraction are picked when naming the blob and can be changed from its ID card.
