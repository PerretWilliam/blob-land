Blobs share new moments. A kind blob cheers up a friend who's feeling low, a blob with a deep crush finally confesses (and it may or may not be mutual), friends tease each other and rivals take jabs, a blob shows off what it just found, and a tired couple or pair of best friends naps side by side. Each has its own little show and journal line. The garden now knows every blob's character, ready for the player to shape their own.
