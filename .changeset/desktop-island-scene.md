Full-screen isometric island: blobs walk, hop and sleep in their nest under a day/night sky, with their current activity shown by their name. Click a blob to follow it. Edit your private island tile by tile (grounds, roads and rivers that join up on their own, hills and ramps, trees, bushes, rocks and cacti) at any size from 2×2 to 16×16. A journal lists what your blob did over the last three days.
