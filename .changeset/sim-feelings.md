Who a blob is with shows on its face and in its mood: chatting with its sweetheart it's starry-eyed, a crush makes it shy, a rival keeps it awkward, and a good time with a sweetheart or a best friend lifts its mood more. A meeting's mood usually carries into what it does next (anger always does), love and anger fade slowest, and mood steers behaviour: cheerful blobs explore and seek company more, low ones sit a while.
