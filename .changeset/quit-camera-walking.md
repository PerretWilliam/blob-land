The game can be quit from the main menu and the in-game menu (closing the window still lets your blob live on in the tray). Opening a blob's relations no longer drops it from the camera, and letting go of a blob eases the view back a little instead of snapping to where it was. Clicking a blob from far away now pans to it first, then zooms in smoothly. In the garden, blobs no longer dash across the map or run along walls to reach a far-off spot: they walk round lakes and cliffs at a stroll, and hop or waddle as they should.
