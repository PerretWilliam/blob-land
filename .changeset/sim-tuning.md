Relationships tuned on 90-day simulated gardens: friendship and romance grow slower the higher they are and chemistry weighs more, so best friends are earned (about 5% of pairs instead of 25%); blobs flirt more and romance fades slower apart, so couples form (5 to 13 per 20 blobs in 90 days) and weak ones fade and split after about a month; births are rarer; grudges last a little longer, but rivals keep out of each other's groups.
