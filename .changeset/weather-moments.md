The weather brings its own moments. Blobs huddle under a big leaf when it rains, or splash in the puddles; they have snowball fights and build snow blobs while it snows, pick flowers in blossom time, jump into piles of leaves in the autumn, and watch the fireflies on summer nights. Stargazing needs a clear night now, and the shooting-stars season makes it a favourite.
