Meetings leave their mark: a blob stays angry for a while after a fight and cools off in its own time, a bad meeting leaves it down, a kiss leaves it dreamy, and a real fight weighs more on its mood. The relations panel can be searched by name, filtered by status (click a summary chip) and sorted (warmest, name, friendship, love, tension, most met), and a blob's ID card has a "See relations" button to open anyone's relations.
