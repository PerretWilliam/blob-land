The garden's islands have real relief now: a mountain rising in terraces to a snowy peak and a frozen tarn, a river tumbling down its waterfalls through a lake to the sea, rolling hills, mesas in the desert, cliffs where the mountain meets the sea, woods along the river, groves in the meadows and, here and there, rocky islets offshore. Roads wind from the village to the tarn, the beach, the lake and the oasis, and nests are scattered around rather than lined up. Each island is laid out its own way, so no two regions look alike.
