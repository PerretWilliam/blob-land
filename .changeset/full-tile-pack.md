The islands use every piece of their sprite pack. Wide rivers and lakes flow as one sheet of water, rivers freeze where they meet ice, run under roads and tumble down slopes as waterfalls, and hills get smooth corners. Roads bend rounded or square. A new Bridge tool in the island editor lays a bridge across a river, and all eight of the pack's clouds drift by, grey over a snowy island.
