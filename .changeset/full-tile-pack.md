The islands use every piece of their sprite pack. Lakes and coasts get rounded shores, wide rivers flow as one sheet of water, rivers freeze where they meet ice, run under roads, and fall as a waterfall on their own wherever they meet more river a block up. Hills get smooth corners and roads bend rounded or square. The garden shows all of it: a river from a frozen tarn down waterfalls to a wide mouth, rolling hills, a lane over a culvert. Nests are now cosy woven nests lined with straw. A new Bridge tool in the island editor lays a bridge across a river, and all eight of the pack's clouds drift by, grey over a snowy island.
