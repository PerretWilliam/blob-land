The islands use every piece of their sprite pack. Lakes and coasts get rounded shores, wide rivers flow as one sheet of water, rivers freeze where they meet ice, run under roads, and fall as a waterfall on their own wherever they meet more river a block up. Hills get smooth corners and roads bend rounded or square. The garden is redrawn by biome: a mountain at the back with a road up its terraces, a desert, a meadow with a pond and a forest with a lake, split by the roads from the village and growing with the island. A river comes down from a frozen tarn in waterfalls to a wide mouth. Lakes now show whole. Nests are now cosy woven nests lined with straw. A new Bridge tool in the island editor lays a bridge across a river, and all eight of the pack's clouds drift by, grey over a snowy island.
