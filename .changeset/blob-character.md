Blobs have more character. Three new traits, kindness, loyalty and curiosity, join the five they had: curious blobs wander and find more, kind ones pick fewer fights and make up sooner, and loyal ones take their time falling in love but stay together through more. Personalities now shape who gets on: playful blobs click, two hotheads clash. A blob in a couple flirts less, and if it does, its sweetheart may not like it. And a best friend is special now: one each, two for the most sociable, and a closer friendship can take that place.
