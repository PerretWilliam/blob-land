Early birds and night owls: every blob has a chronotype that moves its night by up to 4 hours either way, and it sleeps until its own morning, so the garden is rarely all asleep (in a 20-blob garden, 8 to 11 are still up at 21:00 UTC and a few past 23:00, where none were before). Blobs from before get a chronotype rolled once and stored on the next world step (or next launch, for the private blob).
