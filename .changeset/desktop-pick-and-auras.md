Blobs hidden behind others are easy to reach: click a name to select its blob, or click again where blobs overlap to go to the next one behind; the selected blob comes to the front. Newborns sparkle for a while, and a blob just out of a couple carries a broken heart (`/garden` flags them as `heartbroken`).
